# Accounting chart and dates: migration contract

## Current recording

New period closes use chart version 8, São Paulo accounting calendar version 2, and cutoff policy version 2. The chart separately reports customer deposits, loan and credit-card receivables, paid-in synthetic capital, retained synthetic earnings, fee and interest income, loan expected-loss allowance and expense, and operational loss. New ledger entries have immutable posting, value, and business dates. Older ledger entries retain `legacy_unknown` value-date provenance.

The synthetic opening capital contribution is a balanced cash-asset debit and equity credit. Each daily close stores its prior-day trial balance, then posts a balanced result-transfer transaction on the following São Paulo posting date that clears physical revenue and expense accounts and moves the net result to retained earnings. Loan interest due and unpaid is accrued to a receivable and income; later payment clears the receivable instead of recognizing income twice. The expected-loss provision is an explicit, reversible, loan-referenced posting under the simulation policy. These are simulation accounting rules, not a regulatory capital or COSIF certification.

## Historical close policy

1. A close keeps its original chart, calendar, cutoff versions, and trial-balance bytes. Upgrades do not restate it to the current chart.
2. Each new close stores an immutable SHA-256 seal over its close metadata and exact trial-balance snapshot in the same transaction. On startup and repeat close, the seal and snapshot structure are checked. A close on the current chart and cutoff also has its date-cutoff projection recalculated.
3. On first upgrade, an older close with a structurally valid trial snapshot gets `legacy_imported` provenance. Its local seal detects later changes, but cannot prove the state before import. A close without a trial snapshot gets `legacy_missing` provenance and cannot be replayed as verified. An invalid snapshot aborts the seal migration atomically.
4. The seal is in the bank SQLite database. The campaign runtime also records each new close hash in `events.sqlite` before the bank commit and confirms it after the commit. A pending preparation is resolved on restart as committed when the bank close matches, or aborted when the close never committed. A mismatched or missing external record blocks startup and repeated close verification. Once registered, the bank refuses a later close without the event-store hook, and a replacement event store cannot silently reimport the bank history. Existing closes are imported into the event store with explicit `legacy_imported` provenance; a close with a missing trial snapshot cannot enter this runtime. This independent SQLite file catches resealing of the bank file, but the two files still have the same local administrative owner and are not WORM storage. The ledger has its own event-store/file anchor with the same administrative-owner limit.
5. A period-close snapshot captured before a chart upgrade remains byte-for-byte unchanged. New closes use the current version. Historical display must show the recorded version and provenance, not silently present a restated balance.

## Remaining acceptance

The event-store close anchor has direct preparation/confirmation crash tests, local resealing and missing-anchor tests, a restored bank/event-store pair test, a historical import test, missing-event-store denial, and repeated-close verification. New round snapshots carry the committed close-anchor count and verify the bank/event-store pair read-only after copying and on restore. An explicit restore test deletes the external anchor and updates the file hash in the manifest; verification still fails. Legacy snapshots without an anchor field remain readable and are cross-checked when their database pair contains anchor state. The focused accounting, runtime, and snapshot selection passed 35/35; `pnpm check`, `pnpm build`, and `git diff --check` passed. The complete unfiltered suites and Audit 3 gates remain open. Independent administration of the event store and a same-commit full acceptance run remain separate work.
